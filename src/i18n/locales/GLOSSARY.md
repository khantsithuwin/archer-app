# English–Burmese terminology

Use these terms consistently in product copy and translation resources.

| English | Burmese UI term | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Archer | Archer | Brand name; do not transliterate. |
| Email | Email | Keep the familiar technical term; add Burmese context such as `လိပ်စာ` when needed. |
| API, URL, OTP | API, URL, OTP | Keep the standard Latin abbreviations. |
| USD, MMK | USD, MMK | Currency codes are never translated. |
| client | အလုပ်ရှင် | A person or organization offering work. |
| freelancer | လွတ်လပ်ပညာရှင် | An independent professional seeking work. |
| job | အလုပ် | Use `အလုပ်အခွင့်အလမ်း` when emphasizing an open listing. |
| proposal | အဆိုပြုချက် | A freelancer's private application/offer. |
| contract | စာချုပ် | The recorded agreement after a proposal is accepted. |
| milestone | အလုပ်အဆင့် | A defined delivery stage within a fixed-price contract. |
| workspace | အလုပ်ခွင် | The signed-in management area. |

Prefer natural Burmese sentences over word-for-word English structure. Burmese remains left-to-right and must not receive artificial letter spacing or forced uppercase styling.
